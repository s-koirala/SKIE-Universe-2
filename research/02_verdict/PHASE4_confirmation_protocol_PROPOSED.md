# Phase-4 allocator-confirmation protocol — PROPOSED (not authorized)

*Drafted 2026-07-19. **STATUS: `proposed` — Phase-4 entry is NOT authorized.**
The 2026-07-19 authorization covered the first-admission VERDICT only;
per the ADR-0016 D6 principle no stage runs on its predecessor's
authorization, and absence of a record is "undecided", never implied
consent. This draft exists so the operator's Phase-4 gate decision is made
against a written specification (the ADR-0016 stage-A idiom). Nothing here
runs, registers a trial, or selects anything until a named, dated operator
authorization of Phase-4 entry exists.*

## 1. Entry condition and object

- **Entry:** arsenal ≥ 2 admitted members (ADR-0007/roadmap Phase-4
  condition) + the named operator authorization.
- **Object:** the ADR-0007 confirmation ladder over **(allocator-config ×
  Kelly-fraction)** on geometric growth (ADR-0013), net of switching
  costs, on the shadow ledger — producing (if any configuration survives)
  the **ADR-0012 standing incumbent champion** = the live-capital anchor.
  Champion certification and the ADR-0010 ramp remain separate operator
  gates; the F stop rule of the 2026-07-19 session precedes both.

## 2. Registered-before-run design (to freeze at authorization)

- **Allocator-config grid (registered BEFORE any run; no config added
  after):** the static rungs (EW, inverse-vol) as baselines plus the
  dynamic session-cadence selection family the program's thesis names
  (fixed-share / Herbster-Warmuth-style switching over admitted experts;
  exact parameterization enumerated and REGISTERED at freeze, each
  (config × Kelly-arm) pair a distinct trial). Dwell/switching feasibility
  constraints from H900 bind the grid (no sub-dwell switching configs).
- **Estimand:** realized allocator geometric growth per session, net,
  switching costs INSIDE the stream (allocator-level turnover costed at
  the ledger cost model when member weights change).
- **Ladder:** the same five benchmarks, non-degenerate at arsenal size ≥ 2;
  inference: one Hansen SPA per benchmark over the registered
  (config × Kelly) family (the FROZEN `hansen_spa` estimator, reused) +
  `romano_wolf_stepm_panel` identification + DSR/PBO at the then-current
  cumulative register surface + the non-waivable matched-turnover rotor.
- **Multiplicity:** trials append to the accepted
  `allocator-confirmation-kelly-sweep` family (its registered scope; the
  VERDICT-1 §6 reconciliation keeps the (config × Kelly × pool) family
  single); the §A.8(3) criterion re-decision applies to THIS family's
  measured dependence at its first swept scan.
- **Kelly-winner selection:** happens HERE and only here — data-selected
  by best geometric growth over the joint (config × arm) space, and ONLY
  if the D(a) first-admission Kelly concavity/ruin re-instantiation froze
  cleanly (its §B standing condition); otherwise selection stays blocked
  and the sweep is reported unselected.
- **Windows/spans:** the registered rolling family; span admissibility per
  the VERDICT-1 §3 rule (same-cycle reuse; register-conserved
  multiplicity); burned mirror per window under
  `purpose: allocator-confirmation`.
- **Champion:** the surviving configuration (if any) becomes the ADR-0012
  incumbent; the DETHRONING rule stays NON-OPERATIONAL pending
  `P1-2.0-CHAMPION-MARGIN-DWELL-CALIBRATE` — immaterial for a FIRST
  champion (nothing to dethrone), material from the second challenger on.
- **Labeling:** every artifact carries the substrate grade of its members
  (an H105-containing configuration inherits CONDITIONAL-GRADE until the
  DR-repaired re-adjudication); Tier-0 cost labels throughout.
